Money Forecast Privacy Policy
Effective date: November 19, 2026
Overview
Money Forecast ("the app") is a budgeting and cash forecast tool. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: the information you enter stays on your device, and we do not collect it.
Information you enter
The app lets you enter financial details such as account balances, income, bills, credit card information, and debt details. This information is stored only on your device. We do not have access to it. We do not receive it, copy it, or store it on any server.
Information we do not collect
• We do not require an account, sign-in, or email address.
• We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
• We do not connect to your bank or credit card accounts.
• We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.
• We do not sell or share your information with anyone.
Internet use
The app does not send your data over the internet. The charts in the app are drawn by a library that is bundled inside the app, so no outside website is contacted to show them.
Backups and exports
The app may let you export or back up your data. Any file you create is saved where you choose, and you control who sees it. Your device may also include the app's data in its own Android or Google device backup, depending on your phone's settings. Those backups are managed by Android and Google under their own privacy policies, not by us.
Deleting your data
You can delete all of the app's data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the app.
Children's privacy
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
Not financial advice
The app provides estimates based on the information you enter. It is not financial, tax, or legal advice.
Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
